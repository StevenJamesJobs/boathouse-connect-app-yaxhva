The Poster for Menu items & Recipes — B2

Theme

One shell for everything: the s81 Poster (GlassHeroSheet, hero ≈46 %, title set INTO the photo, pills only, photo pager, blur wash + title block + glass panel sliding over the pinned hero). P1 / P2 are the two ways to carry a menu item's price and Redeem — pick one. R is the recipe poster (Bartender Assistant, Menu 1/2 recipes, Cocktails A–Z, purées). N is the no-photo board most menu items will show. W keeps the wine rule (contain on white). Photos page sideways; the dots + "1 / 3" only appear with extra photos (B3).
