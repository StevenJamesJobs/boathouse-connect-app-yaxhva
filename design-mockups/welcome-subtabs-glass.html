The Roster grammar (s83): no wrapper at all — the surface tiles are the only glass, straight on the AmbientGlow. Lightest; the capsule under it becomes the first "edge".
What changes in codecomponents/PortalHome.tsx only: substick loses its background + shadow and gains the MenuCategoryTabs backdrop (opacity from todayScrollY / eventsScrollY crossing 6pt); both capsules move to the glass grammar; the Events capsule gets its own onLayout (today it borrows the Today one's height). The calendar passes card: 'transparent' + edgeToEdge (+ the GlassCard wrapper in V1).
AndroidGlassBlur can't blur on Android (s85) — the pinned backdrop paints colors.card at 0.97 there, so Android keeps a frosted near-opaque bar when pinned and is transparent at rest. Same as the Menu today.
Pinned wash (also B1)The Menu's category tabs use the heavy 50→78% wash, which reads as a solid bar on the phone. The Lighter 22→46% setting is the proposal for both: the blur keeps the chips legible over scrolling cards, the glow stays visible. Final alpha gets judged on the device.
