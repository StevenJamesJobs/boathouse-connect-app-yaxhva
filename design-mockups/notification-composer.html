Notification Center — the composer, three ways

The two asks: the Send To picker fills the screen on a big team, and the page needs breathing room / organisation. N1 keeps one scrolling page but splits it into three glass cards and moves the audience into a sheet with a job-title pill cloud. N2 puts the pill cloud right on the page (no sheet). N3 turns the composer into the Content Kit's steps (Audience → Message → Extras → Review).
Pills use the onboarding chip grammar — ring + a fixed ○→✓ slot, never a solid fill — and every list caps at the sheet's 88 % and scrolls.

What changed: the composer becomes the announcements editor's steps — Audience (this screen: All staff, a search field for big teams, the full pill cloud), Message (title · body · translation), Extras (photo · opens to · push), Review (the staff-facing preview + the audience line + Send). Consistent with the rest of Manage, the most taps.
Why the picker filled the screenToday the audience lives in a plain RN Modal with one row per job title and no height cap — twelve titles is already taller than the phone. All three concepts put the titles in a wrapping pill cloud (three to four per row) inside a container that caps and scrolls.
Pill grammarGlass pill, ring in the theme tint when selected, a fixed ○→✓ slot on the left so the row never reflows; All staff is the radio at the top (N1 / N3) or the first pill (N2). The count line under the cloud always reads "N selected · M people" so the sender knows the reach before Send.
The breathing roomN1 fixes the Translation-to-Photo gap by making it a card seam (12 pt + the card's own padding); N2 by eyebrow rules with 16 pt above each; N3 by moving the photo to its own step. The Send button names the reach ("Send to 18 people") in all three.
RecommendationN1 — it keeps the one-page flow you already use, the three cards give the page its order, and the sheet handles any team size without ever touching the page layout. N2 if you'd rather never open a sheet; N3 only if you want the composer to match the announcement editors step for step.
