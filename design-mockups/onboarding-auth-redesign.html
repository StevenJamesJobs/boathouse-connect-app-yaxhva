How should your categories work across your menus?
  • You can switch modes anytime in Settings.
  • Both modes let you feature menu items on the Home screen’s Specials tab.
SharedPer-menu
  • Both menus use the same categories — e.g. your Starters, Lunch, Dinner and Beverages appear on both.
  • Great for Winter and Summer menus whose categories don’t change over the year, or two menus that share categories all day but carry different items.
  • An item can be on only Winter, only Summer, or both — e.g. Mozzarella Sticks on both, Calamari on Summer only.
  • Winter and Summer each get their own independent categories that you edit separately — every item belongs to one menu or the other.
  • Best when your two menus are truly different — e.g. a Breakfast and a Dinner menu with completely different categories.
  • Also great when one menu is always available and the other is a Specials menu that changes weekly or daily.

Notes · decisions I need from you
1 · The default paletteMy recommendation: don’t make MyResto Ocean — make Moonstone match the logo. The login sits right under the logo, so they must agree, and two apps sharing Ocean costs MyResto its own face. It isn’t a seventh theme: it’s a re-tune of Moonstone in place — charcoal #181B21 → logo navy, loud #FF7A2F → the logo’s softer #F99861, light-mode buttons → ring navy #1F2334. Flip the palette buttons and judge Logo vs Ocean vs today on the whole flow. Boathouse stays Ocean light regardless.
2 · Which theme does Login wear?First-timers always get the app default. For a returning device I’d keep the theme saved on that device (themes are on-device already), so Login → dashboard is one continuous look, exactly your goal. The alternative — Login always in the default — means an Emerald user flashes Moonstone → Emerald at every sign-in.
3 · Where owners personalizeMocked as you asked: step 4 of 5, before Review. One thought — it’s the only step about them rather than the restaurant; your “own account, NOT every user” line handles that well, so I’m happy with it there.
4 · The junk-signup worry — what I foundI checked the server (read-only): join-code lookups and employee joins are rate-limited, but owner signup has no limit at all today. The tick-box is a legal and a human speed-bump; it won’t stop a script. I’d add, this session, the same throttle the other two use (a handful of new restaurants per IP per hour) — additive, invisible to real owners. Your call; I won’t touch the server without your word.
5 · The clickwrapOne tick covering “I am the owner” and “I agree to the Terms & Privacy Policy”, with both as live links and the button dead until ticked, is the form courts treat as real consent. To make it count later we should record it: a timestamp + terms version on the new org at signup. Small additive column — can ride this session or wait for compliance. Links point at placeholder myrestoconnect.com/terms · /privacy constants until the site exists.
6 · Email required + duplicatesLive builds 17 / vc11 still send a blank email, and the DB is shared — so the server must keep accepting blank until those builds retire; the new screen enforces it client-side. To actually stop duplicate accounts the join RPC gains an “email already on this team” check (new clients only feel it). Phone + tagline = two new optional params with defaults, so old builds keep working untouched.
7 · “Until they reach the dashboard”Today the device flag flips the moment a session exists. I’ll move it to the first portal mount, per your rule — so someone who quits on the password screen sees Welcome again rather than a Login they can’t pass. (Note 6’s duplicate-email check is what keeps that from minting a second account.)
8 · Password managersWhichever login you pick, both inputs stay mounted with proper autofill hints, so iOS Keychain / Google can fill username + password in one go — when that happens the screen skips straight to the Sign In state.
9 · Boathouse buildNo Welcome screen there (no join codes, no owner setup on that variant — unchanged from today). It gets the new Login only: McLoone’s mark, Ocean light, same motion.
10 · CopyI tidied spelling and grammar in your new lines but kept your voice — read them on the phones; anything you want worded differently, say so now, since each string is minted EN + ES.
