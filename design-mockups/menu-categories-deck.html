Changed since round 1

  • Long-press a tile starts the wiggle and picks that tile up in one motion. The Reorder button stays for anyone who doesn't find the press.
  • Category sheet: Rename, Colour and Built-in (or Delete) sit right under the name. Built-in shows once.
  • Libations rows carry the link icon beside the eye and the ⋯.
  • Delete asks where the items go before anything is removed.

How it behaves

  • Reorder saves when you let go of a tile. Done only stops the wiggle.
  • Hiding a category sends it to Not in use; showing one adds it to the end of the grid.
  • Built-in cocktail subcategories stay linked to recipes. Your own Libations subcategories can be linked or unlinked.
  • The tile drag is built on the gesture and animation libraries already in the app, so nothing new gets installed.
