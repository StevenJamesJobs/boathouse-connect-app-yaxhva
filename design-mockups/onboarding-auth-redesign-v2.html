How should your categories work across your menus?
  • You can switch modes anytime in Settings.
  • Both modes let you feature menu items on the Home screen’s Specials tab.
SharedOne set of categories for both menus
Per-menuEach menu keeps its own categories
  • Both menus use the same categories — e.g. your Starters, Lunch, Dinner and Beverages appear on both.
  • Great for Winter and Summer menus whose categories don’t change over the year, or two menus that share categories all day but carry different items.
  • An item can be on only Winter, only Summer, or both — e.g. Mozzarella Sticks on both, Calamari on Summer only.
  • Winter and Summer each get their own independent categories that you edit separately — every item belongs to one menu or the other.
  • Best when your two menus are truly different — e.g. a Breakfast and a Dinner menu with completely different categories.
  • Also great when one menu is always available and the other is a Specials menu that changes weekly or daily.

1 · Welcome — W-B or W-A?The one design pick left. Both are live above. W-B now uses the exact Settings-tile motion, so it’s a known quantity on both platforms.
2 · Staff choosing their own positions — my honest readGood idea, low risk. I checked how the app uses them: positions only decide which assistants, quizzes, exams, message groups and schedule roles someone sees. Manager powers come from a separate role that sign-up can never set — a joiner is always a plain employee. Worst case someone ticks Bartender who isn’t, and sees bartender quizzes until a manager unticks it. Two guards I’d add: the server only accepts titles from that restaurant’s own list, and the list shown to joiners leaves out any title containing “Manager” (Manager, Bar Manager, GM…) so nobody badges themselves one. OK?
3 · One quiet check at step 2Because the account is now created at the very end, I don’t want anyone to fill five steps and be bounced on the last tap. So Next on step 2 asks the server once: is this email already on the team · what will the username really be (jsmith or jsmith2 — your ssignup2 case) · the positions list. It only answers for a valid join code and shares the join rate-limit.
4 · Email: one per team, or one per person everywhere?Your wording says “already on this team, or in use by another user”. I’d enforce one per team only. Someone who works at both Boathouse and Robinson Ale House needs the same email on two teams — a global rule would lock them out of the second one. Suggested message: “This email is already in use on this team. If this is an error, speak to your manager right away!” Tell me if you’d still rather have it global.
5 · Shared / Per-menu closed by default — what if they never tap?Today Shared is silently pre-chosen. With both chips closed, I’d make Next ask for a pick (“Choose how your categories should work”) rather than save a choice they never saw. It’s one tap and it’s a decision worth a second of thought. OK?
The throttle, clarifiedThe two join calls are already limited (20 tries per 15 minutes per network). The gap was owner signup, which has none — that’s the one I’ll add: 5 new restaurants per network per hour, then an hour’s pause. Generous for you making sample restaurants from home, a wall for a script. Goes in with the build’s server changes, along with recording the Terms tick (time + version) on each new restaurant.
