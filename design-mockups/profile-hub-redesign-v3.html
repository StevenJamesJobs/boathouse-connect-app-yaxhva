Separators with weight: each group gets a tint eyebrow, its count, a hairline that runs to the edge, and 16pt of air above it — twice the row gap — so the eye lands on the break before the next name. Groups are Today · Yesterday · Earlier this week · then month names (September, August…), which also tells you how far back you have scrolled. Same rows as before underneath.

Where themes are savedOn the device, as today: the theme id and the Light / Dark / Auto mode already live in AsyncStorage; a custom accent adds one small record beside them — the hue, the neutral base, and the preset it started from — and the app derives the full light and dark token sets from that at launch. Nothing goes to Supabase: it is a device preference, so a second phone starts fresh (cross-device sync is one column later if you want it).
Hue and base are independentThe slider drives only the accent family (tint, ember, glow, badge blue). The neutral base — Graphite, Navy, Espresso — is chosen on its own and carries its own light and dark neutrals. A preset chip seeds both (Ocean → its blue on Navy; Emerald → its green on Graphite), and you can change either afterwards. The preview always shows the pair together.
Empty state ruleNew accounts start with zero favorites and the invitation card. Accounts that already have quick tools saved keep them, shown as Simple tiles until edited — nothing anyone chose is lost by the upgrade.
Options, two doorsThe options sheet opens from the editor (the Options chip on a checked sample) and from the hub (the ··· on a wide live tile). Simple tiles show no ··· on the hub; their options live in the editor only.
Settings: what the build changesa and b share every piece — the four tiles, the toggle rows, the password fields, the Subscription row. Only the motion differs, so the choice never costs a rebuild later.
