How generation works

Questions — how many to build. Use −/＋ or tap the number to type. You can add, delete and edit after.

Sources — where questions come from: your menus, wine list & pairings, libation recipes, cocktails and checklists. Only what you turn on gets used.

Photo questions — "which dish is this?" style, from items with photos. The ratio sets how many of your questions get one.

Difficulty — Standard keeps it friendly (prices, sections, dietary); Advanced digs into ingredients; Mixed blends both.

Value — Bucks paid per correct answer. Set $0 for a practice quiz with no payout. Any question can override its own value later.

Time limit — the countdown once an employee starts. ∞ means untimed.
