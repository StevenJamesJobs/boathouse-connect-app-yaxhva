Forced change: the pre-portal screen that still wears the login palette moves onto the glow. The org’s name and logo anchor it, the copy says why you’re here, the two fields keep their eye toggles, and Log out stays as the escape. Same two-field rule as today (the starter password is already verified by the login itself).

The password questionKeep the current-password field. The app never stores the password — Remember Me keeps the user row, not the secret — so “prefilled but hidden” would mean writing plaintext to the phone, and the field is what stops a borrowed unlocked phone from locking its owner out. The lock-out worry is answered on the field itself: “Forgot it? A manager can reset it from your employee card.” (That reset already exists on Employee detail.)
Employee Hub · EditorRetire both pages. The Manage page’s Employees pane already carries the roster, Add, Share the code, Show inactive and the edit path; the Team tile (row 2) and a “Team” quick-tool point there, Schedules points at the Schedules page. Two Manage links still aim at the editor — they move to the pane. The screen-count on the tracker drops by two.
TaglineA new users.tagline column (60 chars, nullable) and the profile-info RPC widened to accept it; shows on the identity card and on the mini profile card now, and is ready for the Shout-Out Wall. Nothing else reads it yet.
Favorites capToday MAX_QUICK_TOOLS is 4. B’s grid reads best at 4 or 6 (pairs); C’s dock takes 6–8. The catalog itself gains the new families — Tips, Quizzes, Game Hub, Approvals — so favorites can finally point at the pages people actually use.
Theme candidatesFrom the retired 14: Midnight (you asked), Emerald, Gilded port cleanly to glass; Neon Noir, Watermelon and Wildflower fight the glass (the accent has to carry text). Ember is new. Each theme = one light + one dark token set; the preview and the theme cards read real tokens, so nothing lies.
Custom accent — should we?Yes, as a hue not a palette: one slider, three neutral bases, derived tokens, contrast clamped. Free-form colour pickers are where themes fall apart (and where Ocean-blue text on Ocean-blue glass comes from). Ship presets first, the slider behind them.
