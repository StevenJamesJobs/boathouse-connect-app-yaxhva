Both rows become rails like the Connect bar: categories as segments inside one capsule (active = a tinted segment in the category colour), subs as text tabs inside a second. Two blur views total, the cheapest; the category chips lose their individual glass tiles.
Code shape (Welcome)PortalHome.tsx: `substick` loses its background, shadow and (in the new version) any backdrop; the capsule becomes a glass surface with its own GlassBlur; the Events capsule gets its own onLayout. Calendar = `card: 'transparent'` + `edgeToEdge`, no wrapper (V2).
Code shape (Menu)MenuCategoryTabs.tsx: the whole `backdrop` layer (GlassBlur + wash + hairline + the showBackdrop fade) is deleted; chips/pills/rails become glass surfaces with their own blur. Both the user Menu and the Editor get it (one component). `syncBackdrop` and `backdropOn` go away in both hosts.
The top edgeWith no band, a card scrolled under the Menu rows is cut at the header's bottom edge. "Hard cut" is literal; "Soft fade" adds a 28pt bg→transparent gradient pinned at that edge (not a bar: it only kisses the card's top). The Welcome page needs neither — the Connect bar's edge does the job.
