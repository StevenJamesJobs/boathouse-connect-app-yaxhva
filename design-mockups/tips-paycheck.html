Paycheck entries — G

Theme

A paycheck is a journal entry with kind = paycheck: date, amount, optional note. It counts in the weekly Total and the Nightly take chart, never in shift count, $/hr or Tip average. J-A / J-B are the two ways to add one from the Journal day: one dashed button that opens the log sheet with a Shift | Paycheck switch, or two dashed buttons side by side. S is the sheet in Paycheck mode. D is the Dashboard with a payday in the week. Money stays emerald; paychecks are the outlined variant of the same green.

Week total carries a Tips · Paycheck split line under the number; Per hour and Tip average keep using shifts only; Best day stays tips. The Friday bar stacks the paycheck as the outlined segment above the tips so paydays read at a glance. (The Tools tile and the My Hub Favorites tile use the same week total.)
ModelShiftEntry gains `kind?: 'shift' | 'paycheck'` (absent = shift, so every stored entry stays valid) and `note?: string`; a paycheck stores its amount in `tips` so `entryTotalTips`, rollups and the chart include it with zero new math. Exclusions: `shiftCount`, `perHour`, `avgTipPct`, the Tools tile's `weekShifts`, the Favorites `avgPerShift`, the "a double" label.
Open choiceJ-A (one button + switch in the sheet) vs J-B (two buttons). Both use the same sheet. The Journal's "Log Shift" top button stays shift-only either way.
